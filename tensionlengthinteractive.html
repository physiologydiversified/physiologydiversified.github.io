Mini Glossary

Sarcomere
The smallest contractile unit within a muscle fiber. It consists of overlapping actin and myosin filaments whose interaction produces muscle contraction.
Force–Length Relationship
A principle describing how the force a muscle can generate depends on its length at the time of contraction. Peak force is generated at an optimal resting length.
Active Tension
The force produced by contractile elements (sarcomeres) of the muscle during voluntary contraction.
Myofibril
A long, filamentous organelle found within muscle cells, made up of repeating sarcomeres. Myofibrils bundle together to form muscle fibers.
Optimal Length
The sarcomere length at which there is maximal overlap between actin and myosin, allowing for maximal force production.
